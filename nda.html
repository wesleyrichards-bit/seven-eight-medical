Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is dated .

Parties

Each is a “Party” and together the “Parties”.

Background

A. Seven Eight Medical is developing audio technology for operating theatres, surgical patients and recovery settings (the “Project”).

B. The Parties wish to share confidential information for the purpose of evaluating a possible collaboration, investment, supply or licensing arrangement relating to the Project (the “Purpose”).

C. Each Party may disclose and receive Confidential Information, and agrees to protect it on the terms below.

1. Confidential Information

1.1 “Confidential Information” means all information disclosed by or for a Party (the “Discloser”) to the other (the “Recipient”) in connection with the Purpose, in any form, before or after the date of this Agreement. It includes product concepts, designs, drawings, specifications, prototypes, software, clinical requirements and workflow insight, research, prior-art and patent searches, business plans, pricing, supplier and customer information, and the existence and terms of the Parties’ discussions.

1.2 Information belonging to Seven Eight Medical’s founders, directors or advisers and disclosed by Seven Eight Medical is treated as Seven Eight Medical’s Confidential Information.

2. Obligations

2.1 The Recipient shall:

  1. keep the Confidential Information secret and use at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care;
  2. use it only for the Purpose;
  3. disclose it only to its employees, officers and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality duties no less strict than these (“Representatives”), and be responsible for their compliance;
  4. not copy it except as reasonably needed for the Purpose;
  5. not reverse engineer, analyse or test any prototype, sample or software supplied, except as the Discloser agrees in writing.

2.2 The Recipient shall not use the Confidential Information to develop, or help any third party develop, a product, service or patent application that competes with or is derived from the Project.

2.3 The Recipient shall not file any patent, design or trade mark application that uses or claims any part of the Discloser’s Confidential Information.

3. Exceptions

Clause 2 does not apply to information that the Recipient can show by written records:

  1. is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement;
  2. was lawfully in its possession before disclosure, free of any duty of confidence;
  3. is received from a third party entitled to disclose it without restriction; or
  4. is independently developed by people with no access to the Confidential Information.

4. Required disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, court order or a regulator. Where lawful, it shall first tell the Discloser promptly and disclose only what is legally required.

5. Intellectual property

5.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No licence or other right in any patent, design, copyright, trade mark, know-how or other intellectual property is granted or implied, except the limited right to use the information for the Purpose.

5.2 Any improvement, invention or feedback that the Recipient makes using the Discloser’s Confidential Information shall be disclosed to the Discloser and shall belong to the Discloser, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

6. Non-solicitation and non-circumvention

For the term of this Agreement and 12 months after it ends, the Recipient shall not use the Confidential Information to approach, deal with or circumvent the Discloser’s suppliers, manufacturers, clinical partners or investors in connection with products similar to the Project.

7. Return and destruction

On the Discloser’s written request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and confirm this in writing. It may keep copies required by law or held in routine back-ups, which stay confidential.

8. No obligation and no warranty

Neither Party must disclose any information or enter into any further agreement. Confidential Information is provided “as is” without warranty of accuracy or fitness. Nothing here creates a partnership, agency or joint venture.

9. Remedies

Damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach. Each Party may seek an injunction, specific performance or other equitable relief, in addition to any other remedy.

10. Term

This Agreement starts on its date and lasts two (2) years. The confidentiality and use obligations survive for five (5) years after it ends, and for trade secrets for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

11. General

11.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject and replaces earlier discussions.

11.2 A change is valid only if in writing and signed by both Parties. A failure or delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver.

11.3 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other’s written consent, except that Seven Eight Medical may assign it to a group company or to a successor of the Project.

11.4 If any provision is invalid, the rest remains in force.

11.5 No third party has rights under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

11.6 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

11.7 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above or by email to hello@seveneightmedical.com (Seven Eight Medical) and (the Counterparty).

12. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement and any dispute arising from it are governed by the law of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
