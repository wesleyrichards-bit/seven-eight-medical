Operating theatres are loud.
Nobody can touch their ears.

Power tools, alarms and crowded rooms put staff hearing at risk and blur vital instructions. Sterile hands can’t adjust a device. And awake patients hear it all.

One platform · three uses

Designed for every
person in the room.

Theatre team

Protect and talk.

Turns down harmful noise while keeping voices and alarms clear, so the team stays connected.

Awake patient

Calm, but reachable.

Music or guided relaxation during procedures under local or regional anaesthesia, with the care team always able to be heard.

Recovery & wards

Rest, not isolation.

Quiet for noisy recovery bays, without cutting patients off from the nurses looking after them.

Design principles

Ceramic calm.
Clinical discipline.

Every detail answers to the realities of a sterile field, not to a spec sheet.

01

Hands-free by design

Built for people who can’t touch anything that isn’t sterile.

02

Seamless and cleanable

03

Our founder

Wilana Richards

Theatre nurse and surgical assistant. Trained at Stellenbosch University in South Africa, she came to the UK in 2001 and has since assisted many of the country’s leading consultant surgeons. She holds a Diploma in Tropical Nursing from the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine, and appeared as a theatre assistant on Channel 4’s Embarrassing Bodies.

In 2026 she received a Royal Humane Society Certificate of Commendation: off duty at Ghent Regatta, she spotted a collapsed sculler in the water, raised the alarm and cared for him on the bank until paramedics arrived.

Seven Eight Medical brings her front-line insight together with engineers and manufacturers to build audio that belongs in healthcare.
